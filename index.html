Our Curriculum

The School of Tomorrow PACE System

ABCA uses the Accelerated Christian Education (ACE) curriculum — also known as the School of Tomorrow system — a proven, individualized approach to learning that allows students to progress at their own pace while receiving personal attention and spiritual guidance.

Students work through clearly structured learning materials at a pace suited to their ability and readiness.

Individual progress is monitored by teachers, ensuring no student is left behind or held back unnecessarily.

The curriculum builds independent learning habits, self-discipline, and personal responsibility.

Academic growth is balanced with character formation and Biblical values at every level.

Regular goal-setting and assessments help students take ownership of their education.

Individualized Learning

Every Student. Their Own Path.

The PACE system recognizes that every child is unique. Rather than moving an entire class at the same speed, each student sets personal academic goals and progresses when they are truly ready — building confidence, mastery, and a love for learning.

Our Mission & Vision

Guided by faith and committed to excellence, ABCA pursues a clear and purposeful mission for every student.

Mission

Bringing Children to Christ

ABCA is called to lead children to a saving knowledge of Jesus Christ and to disciple them through quality Biblical education. We seek to cultivate each student's God-given potential so they grow into mature, faithful Christian leaders who positively influence their communities and the world — embodying the five E's of our school community.

Vision

A Church-Based Ministry of Education

We envision ABCA as a thriving church-based educational ministry — a place where children encounter Christ, find their identity in Him, and are equipped for a lifetime of Christian service. Our vision is to strengthen families through Biblical education and send forth students who are grounded in faith, capable in learning, and ready to serve.
