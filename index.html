Our School

A Christ-Centered Place of Learning

Almanza Baptist Christian Academy is a Christian, church-related school committed to leading children to Jesus Christ and developing them through quality Biblical Christian Education. Using the School of Tomorrow Accelerated Christian Education PACE system, students follow an individualized, self-paced approach to learning, progressing through their academic material step by step while teachers guide and supervise their growth.

Biblical Foundation

Christian values and Biblical principles are woven throughout every subject and aspect of school life.

Individualized Learning

Each student progresses at their own pace through the PACE system, building responsibility and independence.

Church-Based Ministry

ABCA serves as an educational ministry of Almanza Baptist Bible Church, strengthening lives for Christian service.

Academics

Understanding the PACE Learning System

PACE stands for Packet of Accelerated Christian Education, the core of the School of Tomorrow curriculum. Through these individualized academic materials, students learn at a pace suited to their own ability, moving forward only after mastering each unit.

Teachers supervise and assist rather than lecture, encouraging responsibility, discipline, independent learning, and Christ-like character development. Biblical principles are integrated into every PACE, shaping both mind and heart.

Our Mission

ABCA exists to lead children to Jesus Christ for salvation and to develop them through quality Biblical Christian Education. We encourage every student to discover and develop their God-given talents, mature in Christ-like character, and become Christian leaders who can influence the world for Christ through the 5 E's.

Our Vision

To be a church-based educational ministry that brings children to Jesus Christ, strengthens their lives through Biblical teaching, and prepares them for a lifetime of Christian service and leadership.
